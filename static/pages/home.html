    How it works

    1. Nightly, it scans ~40 liquid markets and logs a forecast for each — direction, expected move, confidence, due date.
    2. When a forecast comes due it's graded against the real close. Nothing is hand-waved.
    3. A learned model (fit on the accumulated history) makes the call; live fundamentals and news add a small, capped nudge on top.
    4. Every change is measured against the previous version on five years of walk-forward history.
